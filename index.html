The philosophy

Made Slowly.
Loved Always.

YARNELLE is a small crochet studio built around the quiet charm of handmade things. In a world moving at digital velocity, our studio practices deliberate slowness. Every stitch adds character, every tension shift reveals human touch, and every creation is designed to be cherished over years, not seasons.

“There is something truly sacred about holding an object that took dozens of quiet hours to weave. You aren't just holding yarn; you're holding someone's focused time.”

Christina Susan Boby • Founder & Artisan
